Hi there, my name is Eve Ainembabazi

I care deeply about helping people eat better without stress, confusion, guilt, or extreme dieting. I created Eat Better Everyday because I know how frustrating it can feel to constantly wonder what to eat, whether healthy food is too expensive, or why it is so easy to start healthy eating and then give up along the way.

I truly believe healthy eating shouldn't feel like punishment; it should feel realistic, supportive, and possible in everyday life.

The goal of Eat Better Everyday is to make healthy eating:
Simple • Affordable • Realistic • Kind to your body

Whether you want to lose weight, gain weight, improve your energy, or simply feel better in your body, you are welcome here.

Eating Better Made Simple

If you are beginning your healthy eating journey, start here.

🥗
The Basics

(Click to flip)

Aim for balanced meals. Include a carbohydrate (rice, potatoes), a protein (beans, eggs, meat), and vegetables or fruit.

💰
Affordable Ideas

(Click to flip)

Breakfast: Oats, eggs, or porridge.
Lunch: Rice & beans, posho & greens.
Supper: Matooke & G-nut sauce.

📉
Weight Loss

(Click to flip)

Do it safely. Eat regular meals instead of starving. Reduce oil and sugar. Slow, realistic progress is best.

📈
Weight Gain

(Click to flip)

Eat more frequently (3 meals + snacks). Include proteins (milk, eggs) and energy foods (rice, potatoes).

⏰
Schedules

(Click to flip)

You need consistency. A simple routine: breakfast, lunch, and supper at predictable times.

💧
Hydration

(Click to flip)

Try to drink 6-8 glasses of water a day. It supports digestion, skin health, and energy.
